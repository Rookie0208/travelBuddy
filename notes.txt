// excel template format 
#	Column	Details
A	Date	Expense date (YYYY-MM-DD)
B	Time Added	Full timestamp when expense was entered
C	Title / Description	Expense name
D	Category	FOOD, TRANSPORT, STAY, etc.
E	Paid By	Who paid
F	Total Amount	Full expense amount
G	People Sharing	Count of members with non-zero share
H	Split Type	EQUAL or CUSTOM
I	Status	Active / DELETED
J	Added By	Who logged it
K+	Share: [Name]	Per-member share amount
